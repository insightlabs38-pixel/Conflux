# Unattended wrapper policy

Generated invocation prompts prepend an external wrapper to the unchanged normal lane protocol and exact assigned unit. The wrapper forbids questions to the owner, interaction with any other agent/session, autonomous selection or stealing, and scope beyond the lease. Require worktree/branch isolation, focused tests, a clean tree, push, and a schema-valid receipt before reporting success. Preserve output and worktree on QUOTA, AUTH_FAILURE, HANG or implementation failure; quota means PAUSED_QUOTA and bounded 5/15/30/60 minute backoff (or explicit reset plus small offset). A first hang receives a graceful interrupt and one retry with failure context; a second creates HIGH ATTENTION. Integration is serialized, verifies ownership and receipt, rebases latest main, rejects conflicts for human review, runs focused and fast gates, pushes only green main, then marks DONE. Never autonomously rewrite conflicts or continue on failed gates.
