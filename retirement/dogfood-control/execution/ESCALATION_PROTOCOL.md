# Escalation Protocol

Stop when acceptance is ambiguous, authorization/security or concurrency behavior is unclear, normalization/math or a hard invariant fails, data integrity is threatened, or one bounded mechanical correction fails.

Route difficult semantics/root cause/security/concurrency/math to Claude; coherent implementation/integration to Sol; ordinary fine-grained work to Luna. Freebuff gets one GLM attempt and one bounded mechanical correction. DeepSeek via Freebuff requires explicit owner authorization to spend Freebucks. Never retry indefinitely. Record ID, lane/model, worktree, evidence and the concrete decision needed. Keep ATTENTION concise.
