# Stretch Goals

Stretch begins when Core expansion gates are healthy; items may be promoted earlier when prerequisites are green and they do not destabilize the critical path.

## S01 — Pairwise judging + Bradley–Terry
Remaining hard bonus; extends EvaluationPlan cleanly.

## S02 — Judge calibration rounds
Common calibration projects/rubric anchors before live judging.

## S03 — Inter-rater agreement analytics
Expose criterion/ranking disagreement without fake confidence.

## S04 — Assignment simulator
Compare coverage/load/conflicts/expertise/connectivity before activation.

## S05 — Mid-event judge rebalancing
Recover from judge dropout/backlog while preserving connectivity.

## S06 — Blind judging/anonymization
Policy-driven hiding of team/person/institution metadata.

## S07 — Advanced Submission CI
Repo reachability, commit capture, license/link/demo/sponsor evidence checks.

## S08 — Submission/moderation diff
Review exactly what changed between immutable versions.

## S09 — Full policy debugger
Interactive allow/deny trace for a chosen subject/action.

## S10 — Segmented communications
Dynamic audiences such as blocked teams or incomplete judges.

## S11 — Reminder/scheduling engine
Automated deadline/judging/voting reminders using local channels.

## S12 — Team marketplace
Participant-controlled skills/open-role/project matching.

## S13 — Rich technical renderer
GFM, highlighting, LaTeX, Mermaid, footnotes/callouts safely.

## S14 — Sponsor/prize judging
Scoped sponsor judges/evaluations over eligible candidates.

## S15 — Award conflict solver
Propose valid allocations under stacking/exclusion constraints.

## S16 — Generated SDKs
TypeScript/Python clients generated from OpenAPI.

## S17 — Migration adapters
Devpost/generic CSV or comparable real-world import adapters.

## S18 — Configuration history/diff
Readable event/stage/rubric/policy changes.

## S19 — Public static archive
Stable read-only post-event project/result archive.

## S20 — Multi-round workflow presets
Screening→semifinal→final evaluation presets.

## S21 — Feedback release controls
Anonymous/nonanonymous, delayed/approved feedback publication.

## S22 — Advanced accessibility audit
Theme/page contrast/alt/heading/keyboard warnings.

## S23 — Optional GitHub/GitLab/Discord/Slack adapters
External conveniences without hosted dependency.

## S24 — Judge schedule/workload calendar
Time-aware assignment/completion view for organizers/judges.
