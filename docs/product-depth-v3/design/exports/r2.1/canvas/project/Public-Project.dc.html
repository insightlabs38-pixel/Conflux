Northwind Grid Forecaster

Substation-level load forecasting 1–24 hours ahead, 21.5 % better than NWP-Lite at 38 ms on a single CPU core.

Track winner · Climate & EnergyTime seriesPyTorchOpen data
RepositoryDemo
Sofia Marín-Reyes, Adaeze Nwosu, Luca Bianchi, Tomás Ribeiro — Team Northwind, University of Porto

Summary

Grid operators plan dispatch from load forecasts, yet numerical weather models are too slow to refresh per substation. We built a weather-conditioned sequence model that forecasts 412 substations jointly, refreshes every 5 minutes[1], and reduces 24-hour mean absolute error from 0.780 to 0.612 relative to NWP-Lite.

Architecture

Method

For substation i and horizon h we minimize a pinball-smoothed absolute error, and evaluate with

MAEh = 1N Σi=1N | ŷi,h − yi,h |(1)

Drift in the weather embedding is bounded under a fixed horizon, ‖rt‖ ≤ ε (Eq. 2, derivation in [1, §4]).

Evaluation

We compare against persistence and NWP-Lite on held-out substations (Table 1); numbers are 5-seed means with 95 % bootstrap intervals.

Limitations

Performance drops on substations with under 18 months of history; extreme weather regimes are under-represented in training.
