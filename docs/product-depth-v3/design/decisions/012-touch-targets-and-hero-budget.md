# D-012 · Touch hit area and hero height budget
**Status**: accepted (PD-DESIGN-B00-T06). Clarifies V-DENS-1 and V-HERO-1; adds token `--h-touch: 44px`. No board changes.
- Every interactive control on a touch layout (below 768px, and 44px rows on touch at 768-1439) has a hit area of at least `--h-touch`. Measured: the mobile boards draw some 40px controls (`.btn.lg` "Add link", the All / To do / Flag and All / Not in / Issues segmented tabs in MyEvent-Mobile, Judge-Mobile, Onsite-A, Shell-Mobile). The 40px visual may stay only if the hit area reaches 44px; the simplest build is a 44px control. The 56px dominant onsite action (D-006) is unchanged.
- The hero budgets (compact 160-240px desktop, at most 120px mobile) apply to the hero band: art or lockup, title and status, not the one primary-action row beneath. Measured: Public-Event 137px with results published (below the range: the lower bound is not a floor, a smaller header is allowed), Public-Event-Mobile 157px including its 48px action row, Themed standard 300px (limit 360). A hero never grows to carry more content; extra content goes below the fold.
