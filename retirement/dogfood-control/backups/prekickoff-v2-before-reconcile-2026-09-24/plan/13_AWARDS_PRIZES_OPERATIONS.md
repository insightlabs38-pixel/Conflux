# Awards, Prizes and Operations

## Awards
Award is independent of evaluation. Eligibility, winner count, selection source, stacking/conflict rules and manual override evidence are explicit.

## Prize components
cash, credit, discount, subscription, hardware, travel, service, mentorship, swag, other. Never sum non-cash face values into a misleading cash total.

## Fulfillment
Simple pending → contacted → verified → sent → claimed/failed workflow.

## Operations center
Core views operational blockers: unteamed/blocked participants where relevant, submission preflight state, missing artifacts, judging assignment/completion, stage state, publication state and unresolved moderation/abuse signals.

## Communications
Dynamic audiences + in-app/local SMTP baseline. External Discord/Slack adapters are Stretch and never required for offline operation.

## Event launch checklist
Validate critical configuration before opening an event: dates/gates, forms, policies, required artifacts, judging/rubrics, awards, permissions/public pages and obvious contradictory windows.
